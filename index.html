The problem

Food is complicated.
Tracking it shouldn’t be.

Endless logging

Most tools demand constant manual effort. People quit within weeks because the friction is higher than the benefit.

Fragmented advice

Scanners, trackers, recipe apps and fridge lists live in different places. None of them know your full context or your family.

Opaque ingredients

Labels are hard to read in the aisle. Hidden additives, processing levels and personal conflicts stay invisible until it’s too late.

One-size-fits-all scores

Generic ratings ignore allergies, goals, cultural preferences and the people you actually cook and eat with.

The idea

You wear a discreet device. You speak a simple phrase. The rest happens in the background — with transparency, personalisation and almost no ongoing effort.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to log every meal manually?

No. The design goal is voice-triggered sessions and automatic capture. Occasional confirmation may still be needed when confidence is low.

Which devices will it work with?

We are designing for multiple form factors — glasses, pendant and others — so people can choose what feels natural.

Is this medical advice?

No. UNFOOD provides informational support based on publicly available data and the profiles you create. It is not a substitute for professional medical guidance.

How do you handle privacy?

Sensing is session-based. Clear indicators show when the device is active. We prioritise on-device processing and give you control over data retention.

When will it be available?

We are currently in early development. Joining the waiting list is the best way to be notified of private previews and launch timelines.
