The Problem

Tracking food is broken

Most people want to eat better. Almost nobody sticks with the apps. Manual logging is tedious, inaccurate, and abandoned within weeks.

Manual logging fails

People start with good intentions. After a few days of typing every meal, they quit. The data becomes useless.

Hidden ingredients

Packaged food is full of additives, sugars, and ultra-processed ingredients that most people never notice until it’s too late.

No real-time guidance

By the time you look at a calorie count, the decision is already made. You need help at the moment of choice — in the aisle and at the plate.

FAQ

Common questions

When will UNFOOD ship?

We are currently in advanced prototyping. Waitlist members will receive priority access to the first production batch and private beta invitations.

Do I need a phone?

No constant phone interaction required. The wearable handles recognition and voice. Your phone or computer is used only for initial setup and deep insights.

How accurate is the nutrition data?

We combine Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, manufacturer data, and continuous agentic verification. Confidence scores are always visible so you know when data is strong or estimated.

Is my data private?

Yes. We design for privacy first. You control what is stored, and we never sell personal health data. Optional local-only modes are planned.

Will it work for families?

Yes. Multiple profiles, shared household inventory, and age-appropriate guidance are core features from day one.
